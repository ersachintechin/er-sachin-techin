7 Free AI Tools for Students in 2026

Artificial Intelligence is changing the way students learn. Today, students can use AI tools to understand difficult topics, prepare notes, improve writing and create presentations.

In this article, we will explore 7 useful AI tools that students can try for free. Some features may have usage limits.

1. ChatGPT – Your AI Study Assistant

ChatGPT can explain difficult subjects in simple language, help with homework practice, create quizzes and summarize topics. Students can also ask questions in Hindi or English.

Best for: Learning concepts, revision and practice questions.

Example prompt: Explain photosynthesis in simple Hindi with an example.

Visit ChatGPT

2. Google Gemini – Help with Everyday Learning

Google Gemini can help students understand topics, brainstorm ideas, summarize information and prepare study plans.

Best for: General study help and explanations.

Visit Google Gemini

3. NotebookLM – Study from Your Notes and PDFs

NotebookLM helps students work with their own study materials. You can add supported documents and ask questions about their content. It can help create summaries and revision material.

Best for: Studying notes, PDFs and learning resources.

Visit NotebookLM

4. Perplexity – AI Research Assistant

Perplexity helps students explore topics and discover web sources. It can be useful when starting research for a school or college project. Always open and verify the original sources.

Best for: Research and finding information online.

Visit Perplexity

5. Grammarly – Improve Your English Writing

Grammarly checks spelling, grammar and writing clarity. It can help students improve emails, essays and other written work.

Best for: English grammar and proofreading.

Visit Grammarly

6. Canva – Create Presentations and Designs

Canva provides templates for presentations, posters, charts and projects. Its free plan includes useful design features, although some items and tools require a paid plan.

Best for: School projects, presentations and posters.

Visit Canva

7. Microsoft Copilot – AI Help for Everyday Tasks

Microsoft Copilot can help explain topics, brainstorm ideas, summarize information and assist with everyday questions. Available features may vary by account and plan.

Best for: Quick explanations and idea generation.

Visit Microsoft Copilot

How to Use AI Tools Effectively

  • Use AI to understand a topic, not just copy an answer.
  • Ask for explanations with simple examples.
  • Create practice questions and solve them yourself.
  • Check important facts using textbooks and trusted sources.
  • Follow your school's or college's rules about AI use.
Important Tip: AI tools can sometimes give incorrect information. Always verify important answers before using them in assignments or examinations. Never upload passwords, OTPs or private documents.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. Which AI tool is best for students?

It depends on your needs. ChatGPT and Gemini are useful for explanations, NotebookLM for your own notes, and Perplexity for research.

2. Are these AI tools completely free?

Most offer a free plan or free features. Some advanced features have limits or require payment. Check the official websites for current terms.

3. Can students use AI tools on mobile phones?

Yes. Most of these services can be accessed through a mobile browser or their official Android apps where available.

4. Is it okay to use AI for homework?

Use AI to learn, practise and understand concepts. Follow your institution's academic rules and write your own answers.

Conclusion

AI tools can make studying more organized and interactive. Start with one or two tools that match your needs. Use them responsibly and keep learning independently.
